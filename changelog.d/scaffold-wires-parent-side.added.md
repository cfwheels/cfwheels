- `wheels generate scaffold <Child> --belongsTo=<parent>` now wires the other side of the relationship too. It declares the inverse `hasMany(name="<children>")` on the parent model, adds `include="<children>"` to the parent controller's `show` finder, and renders the children on the parent's `show` view inside a marked block that a later scaffold replaces rather than duplicates. Previously the child knew about its parent while the parent knew nothing about the child, so nothing could display the relationship
