- `wheels seed --generate` no longer writes identical filler into different columns. Free-text columns now name the record they belong to and rotate a sentence pool off that label, so two columns called `body` in different tables (`posts.body` and `comments.body`) no longer receive byte-identical values — which made a seeded blog render its comments as an apparent duplicate of the post body. Titles take the owning model's name too (`Post Title 1` rather than `Test Title 1`)
